## Question
After Iter081X defines the exact K5 collision forest and Iter081Y shows that partial K3/K4 counterterm geometry can carry external tangential coefficient functions, is there established spin-foam/GFT authority that already supplies a locality/factorization/cylindrical-consistency law strong enough to turn those functions into uniquely fixed local coefficients?

## Relevant primary/programmatic sources

### Magliaro--Perini, Local spin foams, arXiv:1010.5227
This holonomy spin-foam framework explicitly studies three general requirements: locality, composition law and cylindrical consistency. In its stated scope, cylindrical consistency fixes an otherwise arbitrary **normalization of the vertex amplitude** in Euclidean signature.

This is important independent evidence that cylindrical consistency can constrain spin-foam normalization data. It is not a theorem about distributional extension on Lorentzian Toller collision diagonals, normal jets, or forest counterterms.

### Bahr, On background-independent renormalization of spin foam models, arXiv:1407.7746
Bahr formulates cylindrical consistency of path-integral measures as a background-independent analogue of RG flow equations between discretizations/boundary systems.

This supplies independent motivation for demanding consistency of amplitudes under refinement/coarse graining. It does not provide an operator that assigns values to distributions supported on the simultaneous Lorentz-group K5 collision or fixes the Iter081R invariant jets / Iter081Y partial-stratum coefficient functions.

### Tensorial Group Field Theory renormalization, e.g. Carrozza--Oriti--Rivasseau arXiv:1303.6772
Tensorial GFT develops a genuine renormalization/locality principle based on tensor invariance and proves renormalizability for specific SU(2) TGFT models (including gauge-invariance constraints and local tensorial interactions).

This is strong evidence that nonstandard/group-space notions of locality can organize counterterms in quantum-gravity-inspired field theories. However the cited theorem is a different theory/model class (3D SU(2) TGFT, tensorial interactions/melonic graphs), not the 4D Lorentzian EPRL/KKL causal Toller K5 vertex.

## What can be imported as motivation
The sources justify testing, prospectively, whether a K5 forest extension should obey some combination of:

- graph/subcomplex locality;
- composition/gluing consistency;
- cylindrical/refinement consistency;
- covariance of local counterterms under boundary embeddings;
- a finite family of local couplings rather than arbitrary nonlocal dependence.

These principles are independently motivated; they are not invented solely to rescue the current candidate.

## What cannot be imported as authority
No audited source above supplies, for the present object:

- a local extension operator on the K3/K4/K5 Toller collision strata;
- a theorem that partial-stratum coefficient functions must be constant in the external tangential invariants;
- a map from the Iter081Y coefficient functions to a finite set of couplings;
- a uniqueness/rank theorem on the Iter081R 28-dimensional deepest jet lower bound;
- forest-subtraction coefficients/scales;
- compatibility with Beltran causal branches and the BCG source-order prescription;
- regulator independence.

Therefore it would be an unjustified retrofit to say that `locality` already collapses the partial-stratum function spaces.
