## Scientific question

Does the Ruhl/Toller uniqueness theorem, equivalently implemented by the one-variable Feynman `i epsilon` projector for each Toller matrix, determine the otherwise free common-collision supported extension coefficients of the ten-wedge K5 product?

The candidate answer is **no without an additional genuinely joint multivariable law**.

This gate must distinguish two mathematically different uniqueness problems:

1. **one-wedge Toller splitting:** uniqueness of `t^(+/-)(rho,beta)` as functions meromorphic in one complex spectral variable `rho`, subject to one-wedge asymptotic decay, matching, finite simple Toller poles and `t+ + t- = d`;

No implication from (1) to (2) may be assumed.
