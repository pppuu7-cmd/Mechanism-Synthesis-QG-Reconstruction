A pre-existing CRQN axiom qualifies only if the pre-Iter077Q corpus explicitly supplies **all** of the following, without post-hoc completion:

- **A1_PREEXISTING:** the prescription is actually stated in the frozen candidate corpus, not merely named as an open target/problem.

- **A3_SELECTION_POWER:** it states a rule strong enough in principle to distinguish/select extension data rather than merely demand a property shared by an infinite family.
- **A4_OBJECT_REACH:** it reaches the local causal/K5 amplitude or its extension/renormalization data, rather than only continuum/RG observables after the local object is already assumed defined.
