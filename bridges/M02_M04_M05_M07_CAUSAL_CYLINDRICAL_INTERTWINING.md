# M02 × M04 × M05 × M07 — Causal Cylindrical Intertwining (CCI)

**Status:** `SYNTHESIS_DERIVED_FINITE_SCALE_CONSTRAINT / PHYSICAL_REALIZATION_OPEN`

## Motivation

Spin-foam coarse graining compares amplitudes on different boundary Hilbert spaces using embedding maps. The causal spinfoam programme instead introduces positive/negative causal analytic branches (Toller/Feynman sectors). MSQGR asks for one same-realization condition ensuring that the causal split survives refinement/coarse graining.

## Boundary system

For boundaries `b ≺ b'`, let

- `H_b`, `H_b'` be the boundary Hilbert spaces;
- `iota_b'b : H_b -> H_b'` be an embedding/refinement map;
- `P_b^±` and `P_b'^±` be the causal analytic projectors associated with the two Toller/Feynman branches.

The standard cylindrical consistency condition is

`iota_b''b' iota_b'b = iota_b''b` for `b ≺ b' ≺ b''`.

MSQGR adds the **Causal Cylindrical Intertwining** condition

`P_b'^± iota_b'b = iota_b'b P_b^±`.                 (CCI)

Equivalently, the embedding map sends the positive causal subspace into the positive causal subspace and the negative one into the negative one. It may still mix states within each sector.

## Consequence for amplitudes

Suppose the coarse amplitude is obtained from the finer one by

`A_b = A_b' o iota_b'b`.

Then CCI implies

`A_b o P_b^± = A_b' o P_b'^± o iota_b'b`.

Thus the causal projection can be taken before refinement or after refinement with the same result. This is the finite-scale version of the CARC commutator condition.

## Inductive-limit consequence

If the family of embeddings is cylindrically consistent and satisfies CCI for every comparable pair, then the rule

`P_∞^± [psi_b] := [P_b^± psi_b]`

defines a well-defined causal projector on the inductive-limit state space. The proof is direct: if `psi_b` and `phi_b'` represent the same inductive-limit state, they agree in a common refinement; applying CCI shows their projected representatives also agree in that refinement.

This matters because the causal split is then regulator-compatible rather than a property attached only to one chosen 2-complex.

## Relation to CARC

For a one-parameter or infinitesimal family of scale transformations, differentiating the intertwining condition gives the familiar linearized requirement that the RG generator have no independent cross-sector block. In a fixed identification of the spaces this reduces schematically to

`[P_±, R] = 0`,

or

`P_+ R P_- = P_- R P_+ = 0`.

CCI is preferred as the physical target because actual spin-foam RG maps relate different boundary Hilbert spaces.

## What CCI does not prove

CCI alone does not determine the microscopic vertex, does not prove unitarity, does not prove the existence of a continuum fixed point, and does not establish novelty. It is a compatibility/closure law generated by combining the causal analytic mechanism with the independent spin-foam RG/embedding-map mechanism.
