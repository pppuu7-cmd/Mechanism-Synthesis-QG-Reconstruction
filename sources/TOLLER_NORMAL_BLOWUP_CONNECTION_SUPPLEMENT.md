## Closed form of the leading magnetic shape

Up to one nonzero spin/branch-dependent overall scalar, the recurrence

`C_m/C_(m-1)=-(j-m+1)/(j+m)`

has the primitive solution

`C_m proportional to (-1)^(j+m) binom(2j,j+m)`.

Thus `C_z` is a non-scalar diagonal function of `J_z` for generic `j>=1` (and is `diag(1,-1)` up to scale for `j=1/2`).

The opposite causal branch changes only the common overall leading scale, `C^(-)=-C^(+)`; therefore every conjugation/relative-connection statement below is branch independent.

## Leading matrix over the normal-direction sphere

For a unit boost direction `n`, choose `U_n in SU(2)` with

`U_n J_z U_n^(-1)=J_n`.

Source Eq.(7) gives the leading singular matrix family

`C_n = D^j(U_n) C_z D^j(U_n)^(-1)`.

This is well-defined independently of the section choice `U_n -> U_n exp(phi J_z)` because `C_z` commutes with `J_z`.

Hence the leading singular coefficient is naturally an equivariant matrix-valued object on the sphere of boost-normal directions rather than one direction-independent scalar factor.

## Angular connection of the leading family

For an angular path `U(theta)=exp(theta A)` with `A in su(2)` transverse to the `J_z` stabilizer,

`C(theta)=U(theta) C_z U(theta)^(-1)`

has

`dot C(0)=[A,C_z]`.

The corresponding right-relative connection is

`A_C := C_z^(-1) dot C(0) = C_z^(-1) A C_z - A`.

Because `C_z` is not generically scalar, `A_C` is not generically zero and is not generically the same SU(2) generator `A` times a scalar.

For the ladder generators the exact recurrence gives, on a magnetic basis state `|j,m>` where the shifted state exists,

`C_z^(-1) J_+ C_z |j,m> = -[(j+m+1)/(j-m)] J_+ |j,m>`,

`C_z^(-1) J_- C_z |j,m> = -[(j-m+1)/(j+m)] J_- |j,m>`.

Thus the relative angular connection has nonconstant magnetic weights. The stabilizer direction `J_z` gives zero because `[J_z,C_z]=0`.

## Boundary-intertwiner meaning at source node 5

For the node-5 boost-normal leading family on its four incident wedges, compact covariance gives schematically

`T_e ~ D(U_n) C_{j_e,z} D(U_n)^(-1)`

at leading singular order, omitting the common radial power and branch scale.

The common left `D(U_n)` factors are annihilated by the node-5 invariant intertwiner, as in Iter076W. What remains on the four outgoing magnetic slots is

`F_z := i_5 [tensor_e C_{j_e,z}]`.

Changing the normal direction applies a common right `D(U_n)^(-1)` to these four outgoing slots. Its angular derivative is therefore generated by total `J_x` or `J_y` acting on `F_z`, not directly on the original invariant tensor `i_5`.

Since `tensor_e C_{j_e,z}` need not preserve the invariant subspace, `F_z` need not be annihilated by total angular momentum.
