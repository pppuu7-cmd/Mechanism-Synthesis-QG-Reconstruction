# Targeted literature audit — F9 causal analyticity under spin-foam RG

**Audit date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** targeted search only; **not** a proof of global novelty.

## Question

Is there already a published same-realization result in which the Toller/Feynman causal analytic sectors of a Lorentzian spinfoam are explicitly intertwined with spin-foam coarse-graining/embedding maps and shown to remain closed over RG/refinement steps?

## Causal-analytic side located

1. E. Bianchi, C. Chen, M. Gamonal, **Causal spinfoam vertex for 4D Lorentzian quantum gravity**, Phys. Rev. D 113, 126020 (2026), DOI `10.1103/fwql-t4yr`.
   - causal data encoded with Toller matrices;
   - causally compatible Lorentzian Regge saddles selected in the large-spin regime;
   - single causal exponential / causal rigidity.

2. E. Bianchi, C. Chen, M. Gamonal, **Toller matrices and the Feynman i epsilon in spinfoams**, Phys. Rev. D 114, 046014 (2026), DOI `10.1103/v3kc-4n3n`, arXiv `2604.24945`.
   - positive/negative causal branches defined through analyticity/asymptotics/poles;
   - Feynman `i epsilon` equivalent to the causal projection;
   - Toller matrices are not ordinary Lorentz-group representations, so naive multiplicative local composition is not the correct closure criterion.

## Spin-foam RG / embedding-map side located

3. S. Steinhaus, **Coarse Graining Spin Foam Quantum Gravity—A Review**, Frontiers in Physics 8, 295 (2020), DOI `10.3389/fphy.2020.00295`, arXiv `2007.01315`.
   - RG formulated using boundary Hilbert spaces and embedding maps;
   - cylindrical consistency is central;
   - dynamically determined embedding maps are emphasized;
   - restoration of diffeomorphism symmetry is a principal target.

The review summarizes a large pre-2020 coarse-graining literature and makes clear that the embedding map is not an innocuous bookkeeping choice: it defines which fine degrees of freedom represent a given coarse state and therefore changes the RG flow.

## Targeted-search outcome

Searches combining terms such as `Toller`, `causal spinfoam`, `coarse graining`, `renormalization`, `embedding maps`, `causal projector`, and `cylindrical consistency` returned the two 2026 Toller/causal-vertex papers and the established spin-foam RG literature, but did not surface a paper explicitly proving

`P_b'^± iota_b'b = iota_b'b P_b^±`

for Toller/Feynman causal sectors under a physical spin-foam coarse-graining map.

This absence is only a **search result**, not evidence that no such work exists. Before any publication-level novelty claim, a broader bibliographic audit and expert literature review are required.

## MSQGR consequence

The unclosed intersection is sufficiently specific to use as the next falsifiable research target:

**F9 = causal analytic / Toller sector closure under dynamically justified boundary RG maps in one Lorentzian realization.**

If existing work is later found to establish this, F9 becomes external prior art and cannot carry MSQGR novelty by itself. If not, MSQGR still must demonstrate the relation physically rather than infer novelty from the literature gap.
