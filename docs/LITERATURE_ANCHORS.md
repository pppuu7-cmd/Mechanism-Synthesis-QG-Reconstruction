# Literature anchors for Mechanism Atlas v0.1

This file records literature used only to justify that a mechanism is genuinely present in an established research programme. It does **not** count as evidence that the MSQGR synthesis is correct.

## Causal sets — causality + local finiteness

Sumati Surya, *The causal set approach to quantum gravity*, Living Reviews in Relativity 22, 5 (2019). DOI: 10.1007/s41114-019-0023-1.

Anchor use: M02, M03, characteristic nonlocality, continuum reconstruction problem.

## Loop quantum gravity — background independence + quantum geometry

Carlo Rovelli, *Loop Quantum Gravity*, Living Reviews in Relativity 11, 5 (2008), and later literature.

Anchor use: M01, M04, gauge/constraint structure.

## Spin foams — covariant amplitudes for quantum geometry

Alejandro Perez, *The Spin-Foam Approach to Quantum Gravity*, Living Reviews in Relativity 16, 3 (2013). DOI: 10.12942/lrr-2013-3.

Anchor use: M04, M05, relation between labelled quantum geometry and covariant histories.

## Group field theory — discrete quanta, spin-foam histories, field-theoretic/RG tools

Daniele Oriti and subsequent GFT literature; see also A. Mozota Frauca, *Foundational Issues in Group Field Theory*, Foundations of Physics 54, 33 (2024).

Anchor use: M04, M05, M06, M07, M12 and the possibility of connecting discrete quantum geometry to many-body/continuum phases.

## Causal dynamical triangulations — Lorentzian path integral + continuum/phase structure

J. Ambjorn and R. Loll, *Causal Dynamical Triangulations: Gateway to Nonperturbative Quantum Gravity* (2024; arXiv:2401.09399 / Encyclopedia of Mathematical Physics).

Anchor use: M02, M05, M08, M12; especially the distinction between a Lorentzian microscopic construction and an emergent semiclassical phase.

## Asymptotic safety — RG fixed point / UV critical surface

D. F. Litim, *Fixed Points of Quantum Gravity and the Renormalisation Group*, arXiv:0810.3675; A. Eichhorn, *An Asymptotically Safe Guide to Quantum Gravity and Matter* (2019 publication line).

Anchor use: M07. MSQGR imports the *criterion* of UV criticality, not the asymptotic-safety ontology or a pre-existing beta functional.

## Noncommutative quantum spacetime

J. Aastrup and J. M. Grimstrup, *Intersecting Quantum Gravity with Noncommutative Geometry — a Review*, SIGMA 8 (2012) 018; P. Aschieri and L. Castellani, review of noncommutative gauge/gravity theories (2023).

Anchor use: M11. In MSQGR v0.1 noncommutativity is a possible derived/effective signature, not a required microscopic axiom.

## Holography / quantum information

T. Takayanagi, *Emergent Holographic Spacetime from Quantum Information*, Physical Review Letters 134, 240001 (2025).

Anchor use: M09. The mechanism is treated as a constraint/diagnostic unless the microscopic constitutive entropy/action is itself derived.

## Evidence discipline

The atlas should prefer review-level or primary technical sources and should distinguish:

- existence of a mechanism in a source family;
- mathematical coexistence of two mechanisms in one realization;
- evidence that the mechanism solves a particular closure problem;
- evidence for the new MSQGR synthesis.

Only the last category can increase confidence in an MSQGR candidate itself.
